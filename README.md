# ⚔️ Aryan's Adventure - RPG

A text-based RPG adventure game in your browser! Play as Aryan and embark on an epic quest to save the village of Eldergrove from dark forces.

## 🎮 Features

- **Story-Driven Gameplay** - Multiple story paths and meaningful choices
- **Character Progression** - Level up, gain experience, and improve stats
- **Combat System** - Strategic battles based on your chosen approach
- **Character Stats** - Strength, Intelligence, and Agility affect your success
- **Save/Load System** - Continue your adventure anytime
- **Beautiful UI** - Medieval fantasy theme with responsive design

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser
2. Read the story and make choices
3. Build your stats through combat and leveling
4. Uncover the mystery of Eldergrove
5. Save your progress anytime

## 📖 Story

You wake up in the village of Eldergrove with no memory of how you got there. The village elder asks for your help - hunters have gone missing in the Darkwood Forest.
